How the bot is built

Simple ideas, in easy words:

  1. Brand voice: the bot always speaks as Afridi Trading Japan, in simple polite words. Its promise is: WHAT YOU SEE IS WHAT YOU RECEIVE.
  2. True answers only: the bot only answers from its own knowledge base and database — cars, prices, policies, freight and import rules. If it does not know, it says so and asks a human, instead of guessing a price, a grade or a refund.
  3. Tool actions: the bot can do things — make an invoice, track a shipment, and save your question for a human.
  4. Guardrails: the bot never promises a price or grade it has not checked, never takes money, never changes a price, and sends hard questions to a human.
  5. Human handover: every chat is saved in the database, so the admin can read what the bot said and take over at any time.

No paid API, no vector database, no big AI library — just Python, Flask and a simple keyword brain.
