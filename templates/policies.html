Our Policies

Simple words, from afriditrading.com. If anything is not clear, ask the bot or our sales team on WhatsApp.

1Payment Policy

Pay by telegraphic transfer to our company bank account in Japan, or by Visa, Mastercard or PayPal. We also take WISE business account payments. The beneficiary is always Afridi Trading. Never pay to a person, never use Western Union, and never pay a local agent — our agents cannot take payments. Bank fees are paid by the buyer. Before you pay, verify the person at afriditrading.com/verify. Ask us for the official proforma invoice first, and pay only the amount on that invoice.

2Shipping Policy

We handle the auction, export papers and shipping. Every car shows its delivered price to your port. You collect the car at your port.

3Freight

Freight depends on the car size and the port, so the website does not give one fixed freight price. The exact freight is written on your invoice. The freight prices used in this demo bot are samples, not live quotes — ask for a quote for your car and port.

4Delivery Time

Delivery usually takes 4 to 8 weeks. The exact time depends on the shipping schedule. Once your car is fully paid, we ship it on the earliest available vessel. Ask our sales team for the time to your port, and use your tracking number to follow the ship. We ship by RO-RO and by container, to Europe, Asia, Africa, the Black Sea, the Caribbean, North and South America, Oceania, the Middle East and East Asia.

5Marine Insurance

Shipping is insured, with full marine transit cover. In this demo the delivered price adds $100 for insurance.

6Documents You Get

You get a proforma invoice first. After you pay, we ship the car and courier the original B/L (bill of lading) to you, so you can clear the car at your port.

7Collecting Your Car at the Port

You collect the car at your port. Bring the original B/L and your ID to clear it.

8Refund and Cancellation Policy

The auction security deposit is 100% refundable. If you cancel a purchase order, you may need to pay the costs already spent, because we may have to resell the car. Before you pay, ask our sales team on WhatsApp and get the terms in writing on your invoice. Once a car is bought at auction and shipped, it cannot be sent back, so please check the car, the price and the import rules first.

9Trust

Afridi Trading Japan is a JUMVEA member — an approved Japanese used-vehicle exporter. Japan Car Trust gives a genuine odometer guarantee. Buyers rate us 4.7 out of 5 stars.
